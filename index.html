Who we are

Built by students, for the FIRST community.

“Designed to let members of the community reach out and branch into STEM.”

Lost In Translation is a FIRST Robotics Competition team based in Perth, Western Australia, competing as Team 10342. We were founded in 2024 and took our robot to our rookie FRC season in 2025, backed by Rockwell Automation and Scotch College.

Every season we take on the FRC build challenge from scratch — design, code, wire, and drive a competition robot in six weeks — while growing a team culture built on collaboration, curiosity, and getting more students into STEM.

Outreach

More than a robot.

In FIRST Robotics, outreach is the work a team does off the field — running demos, mentoring younger students, and partnering with schools and sponsors to grow STEM in the wider community. It's judged and awarded alongside robot performance because FIRST's mission is about building people, not just machines. Click a card to see how we put that into practice.
01

STEM Demos

We bring our robot into schools and community events so students can see FRC engineering up close — not just hear about it.
02

Mentorship

Older members mentor newer ones in CAD, code, and wiring — passing down skills the same way they were taught.
03

Sponsor Partnerships

Working with sponsors like Rockwell Automation and Scotch College to fund the build and open doors into engineering careers.
04

Community Growth

Growing our own roster and helping introduce new members to the wider FIRST community every season.
